ORPHEUS  -  BY HAMZA
====================
A music player for your own songs. No ads, works offline, installs on iPhone and Windows.

UPDATING THE APP YOU ALREADY HAVE ON NETLIFY
1. Unzip this file. Open the folder and check that index.html is directly inside it.
2. In Netlify open your site, go to Deploys, and drag the folder onto the upload area.
   Use the SAME site so the link stays the same. Wait for "Published".
3. Open the app once while online. Your songs stay. If the old version is still showing,
   close the app fully and open it again.

FROM NOW ON (every later update)
Upload the new folder to the same Netlify site. Next time anyone opens the app while online,
an "Update" button appears at the top. One tap installs the new version, and the button disappears.
Songs, playlists and settings are not touched. Settings > Updates has "Check for updates".

WHAT IS NEW
- Named ORPHEUS. New lyre icon, and 12 icon designs to pick from (Menu > App icon).
- Songs without cover art get a generated cover drawn from their title. Songs that have art keep it.
  Pick one style or Mixed in Settings.
- 27 themes, plus your own two accent colours (Menu > Themes).
- Shuffle menu: All tracks, Smart shuffle, Newest 50, Random mix of 25, or shuffle one artist.
- Opens from the saved copy, so it starts fast and works with no connection.
- Menu (three lines): Themes, App icon, Settings, with BY HAMZA at the bottom.

IPHONE ICON NOTE
iPhone reads the icon when you add the app to the Home Screen. Choose the icon first.
Changing it later means removing the Home Screen icon and adding it again, which erases the saved songs.

ADDING SONGS
Press Add music and select your audio files (hundreds at once is fine). On iPhone, save songs from
your other app to Files first (Share > Save to Files), then choose them here.
