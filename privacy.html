ORPHEUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

ORPHEUS is a free, ad-free music player that runs in your browser and plays the audio files you add yourself. It is made by Hamza.

What stays on your device

The songs, covers, playlists, likes, themes and settings you use in ORPHEUS are stored on your own device, inside your browser's storage. They are not sent to the developer or to any server of ours. We do not run any server, and ORPHEUS has no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Optional: backup with your Google account

If you choose "Sign in with Google", ORPHEUS asks for one permission: access to its own hidden application folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It can only see files that ORPHEUS itself put in that folder. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

  • What is copied there: your audio files, covers, playlists, likes and profile choices (name and picture you pick inside the app).
  • Where it goes: straight from your device to your own Google Drive. It never passes through the developer.
  • Your Google sign-in token is kept in your browser only, and expires after about an hour.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your data.
  • We do not read your music or your Drive files.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use "Sign out" in the Profile page to disconnect from Google.
  • Remove ORPHEUS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the backup by deleting the app data in Google Drive (Drive settings, Manage apps, ORPHEUS, Delete hidden app data).
  • Removing the app from your Home Screen, or clearing the site data in your browser, erases the songs stored on your device.

Children

ORPHEUS does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
